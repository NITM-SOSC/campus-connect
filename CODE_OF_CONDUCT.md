# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Campus Connect is intended to be a welcoming, collaborative space for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. We, as contributors and maintainers, commit to making participation respectful and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Welcome questions and help newcomers find context.
- Discuss ideas and critique work, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on what benefits the community.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information without permission.
- Follow the project contribution guidance and the rules of the platforms where the community participates.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate disruption, personal attacks, sexualized attention without consent, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable. This includes behavior in project issues, pull requests, code review, and other project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project or its community. Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may remove or edit content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

To report conduct concerns, contact a repository maintainer privately through GitHub. Do not use a public issue for sensitive reports. If you feel unsafe or cannot reach a maintainer, use GitHub's available reporting mechanisms. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical; no specific response timeline is guaranteed.

This document is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The project has not designated a separate enforcement team or published a dedicated contact address.
